Credit Brett Reynolds and contributors, name this project/version, link to the license and indicate changes. See [LICENSE-CONTENT](LICENSE-CONTENT) and the [CC BY 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **Underlying historical texts:** Humboldt’s German text and public-domain historical material retain their public-domain status. Transcription, indexing and inclusion here do not create a claim over the underlying text. Source-specific edition and scan details remain in `sources/manifest.json`.
- **Third-party material:** cited quotations, reference metadata, website extracts and material credited to other authors retain their original rights. They are excluded from this project’s license grants. A reference, local path or checksum does not grant permission to redistribute a source publication. Modern library PDFs and full-text downloads are kept outside the public Git payload.

The work uses AI-assisted drafts, analysis and audits, identified in its provenance records. These licenses grant only rights held by the project contributors; they do not assert that every machine-generated element has independent copyright protection, endorse a model’s conclusions, or certify translation fidelity.

For attribution, use the repository URL and the commit/version you consulted. `CITATION.cff` provides a suggested citation. Contributors should supply material they may share under the applicable license and retain source attribution and third-party exclusions.
