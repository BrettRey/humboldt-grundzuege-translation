## Source preparation and context

1. **Retain the 1906 source as the working base.** Preserve originals, hashes, pagination, section numbering, and the distinction between Humboldt and editorial apparatus. Check the German of each translation unit against the page images and record corrections to OCR.
2. **Investigate the textual basis.** The earlier brief identifies Lindorfer and Trabant’s 2017 critical edition as a priority for comparison. Locate lawful access, verify its bibliographic details, and record consequential differences actually observed. Until checked, describe the work as based on the 1906 printing; do not present it as a newly established critical text. Unavailable material is a recorded limit, not a reason to invent readings.
3. **Build a focused evidence corpus.** Start with holdings already present and relevant writings in the downloaded volume. The earlier discussion identifies Humboldt’s comparative-language programme, work on grammatical form, American-language studies, Chinese-language discussion, correspondence, and later formulations as leads. Historical dictionaries and dated examples supply additional lexical evidence. Retrieve material to answer specific questions; record passages and page references before using them. Sources named in the old chat are leads until verified.
4. **Read across the work before settling terminology.** Prepare a provisional outline of its argument, recurring expressions, examples, and cross-references. The full OCR can support discovery, but passages used as evidence require checking. Distinguish early and later formulations rather than harmonizing them automatically.

## Translation policy

Write readable scholarly English while preserving conceptual distinctions, qualifications, significant repetition, and argumentative dependencies. Preserve paragraph boundaries; divide a sentence when its relations remain clear. Explain difficult concepts in labelled notes. Keep historical evaluations, linguistic examples, editorial corrections, and modern interpretations attributable to their respective sources.

Maintain a small terminology register with occurrences, candidate renderings, evidence, and exceptions. Do not freeze a glossary from the opening paragraph. Theories and dictionaries mentioned in the earlier discussion should inform decisions only after their relevant contents have been read.

## The passage workflow

| Stage | Concrete output and condition |
| --- | --- |
| Freeze the source packet | Checked German, stable passage and clause identifiers, page references, surrounding argument, policy, and relevant evidence. Record uncertainties in the source itself. |
| Generate three separate candidates | Use three distinct model families through existing eligible tools, with fresh contexts and isolated working directories containing only the source packet. Each receives the packet without the current English pilot, another candidate, or translation notes containing its proposed English answers. Briefs emphasize structural fidelity, readable scholarly English, and preservation of conceptual relations. Retain actual prompts, outputs, and model identities. Installed CLI commands are not proof that an authenticated route is available. |
| Compare and investigate | Align candidates by source clause. Record disagreements over scope, referents, modality, meaning, and terminology. Check proposed resolutions against the German and cited contextual passages. Agreement is not a vote that settles a reading. |
| Synthesize | Produce a provisional English version and a short decision record linking consequential choices to source evidence. Keep viable alternatives where the evidence does not decide. |
| Audit separately | Check exact items such as numerals, names, quotations, identifiers, and source coverage with ordinary code where suitable. Use separate source-based model reviews for semantic questions, including omissions, additions, negation, scope, modality, referents, and conceptual distinctions. Label model judgments as such. Fluent English and matching counts do not establish adequacy. |
| Revise and report | Resolve supported findings, retain unresolved ones, and deliver English plus a concise account of changes, evidence, and remaining limits. Brett reviews English and editorial usefulness, without being asked to adjudicate German readings. |
| Revisit affected occurrences | A changed term or interpretation triggers inspection of relevant occurrences elsewhere; avoid automatic global replacement. |
