## Editorial units and context

Use a complete printed section for production, coherence checking and English review. Keep the whole German work available as context. Label uncorrected OCR explicitly; the checked target transcription and verified source evidence govern disputed readings. Paragraph, sentence, word and span identifiers remain the finer units of citation and accountability. A numbered passage may contain multiple printed paragraphs.

## Translation and review

1. Check new German against the scans, recording printed page, PDF page, scan leaf, apparatus and printed paragraph breaks. Preserve earlier checked text and translation versions.
2. Obtain three independent candidates for the untranslated text. Supply the complete German section and broad German context; withhold existing English, glossary answers and other candidates. Freeze outputs and runtime provenance before synthesis.
3. Consult the conditional termbase and earlier English while composing the complete section. Explain consequential choices, rather than deciding by model agreement.
4. Obtain two fresh audits of the entire section’s checked German and proposed English, including argument and terminology across its numbered passages. Require every source sentence to be accounted for and retain individual findings, severity judgments and dispositions.
5. Revise once against the source. Existing text changes create attributed new versions; old bodies and links remain available. Declare new semantic correspondence explicitly. Preserve unresolved alternatives and source-status qualifications.
6. Import through the generic edition format. Validate each prepared import, then perform regression, preservation, reader and publication checks at the section checkpoint. Avoid repeating a full production cycle or human handoff for each numbered passage.

Apply the [footnoting policy](footnoting-policy.md) at each section checkpoint: selective reader-facing notes, recurring explanations in the glossary/commentary, and full provenance in the linked apparatus. Check factual additions against cited evidence and keep Humboldt’s notes, the1906 editor’s notes and translator’s notes distinct. Oxford spelling and avoidance of merely stylistic synonyms are explicit instructions.
