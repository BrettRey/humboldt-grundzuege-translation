# Reassessment of the two existing paragraphs
<!-- SUMMARY: Broader preparation supports retaining current §§1–2; uncertainties remain named and existing versions are preserved · status: completed, no English change · updated: 2026-11-15 -->

The broader reading and historical dictionary checks provide no sufficient reason to revise the present [§1](../../translations/opening-001/revised.md) or [§2](../../translations/paragraph-002/revised.md). This is a source-based coordinator judgement after the preparation, not a new human fidelity approval or another independent translation-candidate cycle. Their earlier candidates, audits, variants, English and notes remain preserved.

## §1

| Choice | Result of broader preparation | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **Continent; study of language** | Geographical grouping and the intrinsic/historical distinction remain separate (§7); the continent does not establish one language family. T02,T29. | Retain. |
| **Converged** | The printed parallel explicitly uses mixture elsewhere. Geographic convergence in the base need not assert that the languages themselves blended. The wider discussion separately treats contact, mixture and inherited connection. T25,T34. | Retain; do not import the parallel's stronger wording. |
| **Language stocks** versus human **tribes, hordes, families** | The source's bearers differ; later whole-language and individual distinctions do not eliminate that difference. T26. | Retain, without modern genealogical certification. |
| **Structure; attained stages of development** | Bau and development are not interchangeable. The comparative essay's organization/development contrast is useful but qualified; Adelung's bilden/ausbilden ranges support conditional formation/development/cultivation rather than mandatory education. T05–06. | Retain development for this graded, attained context. |
| **National distinctiveness; distinctive traits** | Whole-work individuality ranges from sound to language to speaker; it is not one unvarying national essence. T07. | Retain related vocabulary and the local bearer. |
| **Ferment of their powers** | Preserve the metaphor and peoples as subject; Kraft is not simply physical energy. T09,T28. | Retain. |
| **Wearing-down influence; native interaction** | Adelung permits both abrasive removal/smoothing and broader social dealings. It does not establish complete homogenization or narrow commerce. In this sentence the previously introduced foreign material undergoes the influence. T27–28. | Retain; keep this bearer separate from the ferment clause's subject. |
| **Very considerable** for the superlative | The broader terminology work does not resolve the earlier absolute/elative-versus-relative superlative question. | Retain D003's provisional choice; no stronger certainty claimed. |
| **Down to subjugation** for bis auf | Adelung documents temporal endpoint uses of bis auf. This supports the reading's availability, without ruling out every exceptive interpretation of the phrase. T30. | Retain the temporal choice and its existing uncertainty. |

## §2

| Choice | Result of broader preparation | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **Development** of nations; **structure** of languages | The bearers and evaluative levels matter; neither schooling nor mere word formation fits this local Ausbildung. T05–06. | Retain. |
| **In the languages themselves**; **them all** | The wider work often distinguishes national and linguistic attributes, but does not resolve these local anaphoric possibilities. | Retain D005.2 with nations as a recorded possible antecedent. English proximity still tilts interpretation. |
| **Alphabetic writing; literature in the proper sense; a lower level** | Speech-sound inventories elsewhere are not automatically writing. Here the explicit writing restriction, asserted hence and historical ranking remain in the base. T17. | Retain; do not silently modernize the claim or borrow the parallel's omission of the lower-level phrase. |
| **Sameness** versus **similarity** | Later comparison explicitly separates identity, historical connection, shared capacities and likeness. T25. | Retain both rejected sameness and retained similarity. |
| **Sometimes wrongly asserted**; **also been asserted** | Bisweilen supplies explicit frequency; Adelung's wohl has modal, intensive and concessive possibilities. It does not justify automatically repeating the frequency in auch wohl. T30. | Retain the removal of the second sometimes; the precise wohl nuance remains open. |
| **In fact; even in this respect; do not permit** | Keep wirklich and the contrast. Wider reading does not uniquely settle darin's focus or make the German singular erlaubt proof of a different subject. | Retain D005.4–5 and their notes. |

## What this reassessment does and does not establish

The register now gives these choices broader support and explicit exceptions. It does not retroactively turn model agreement into specialist checking. D001's reported cancellation, the unavailable modern critical edition, and manuscript-layer uncertainty remain unchanged. The narrower unresolved points above are suitable qualified decisions for a provisional translation; they are not a reason to reopen an unlimited search.
