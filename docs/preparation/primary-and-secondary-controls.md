## What the primary comparison changes

**Form has no single invariant bearer.** The comparative essay strongly distinguishes organic organization from finer development, then qualifies their separation (IV 2, 8–9). The forms essay asks which expression of a relation deserves the restricted name form (IV 285, 291–292). *Grundzüge* §55 instead contrasts words and discourse-connection as matter/form; §136 separates two forms. Kawi's later definition concerns the recurrent work of making sound express thought (VII/1: 47–49). These are comparisons, not interchangeable dictionary definitions. Evidence: PRIMARY-A02,A04–09; PRIMARY-B11–13; C03,C09.

**Record tension rather than manufacture a developmental story.** The selected forms essay calls its two-class contrast absolute while permitting imperfect realization (IV 310). *Grundzüge* ends by denying a sharply cutting boundary and rejecting rigid classification (V 472–473). The selections establish a difference in formulation. They do not establish its chronology or prove a contradiction across identical criteria; the unread 295–305 gap also limits comparison. For translation, retain each local claim and its qualification.

**Thought, marking and understanding remain distinct.** The Chinese paper separates word meaning from grammatical classification and admits inflection, grammatical words, regular position and determinate implication (V 309–311). It distinguishes conceptual determination from marking of form (318). Thus no inflection is not equivalent to no thought-relations, no structure or no communicative adequacy. Its historical analyses and rankings remain attributed claims, not newly validated descriptions of Chinese. Evidence: PRIMARY-B01–07.

**The comparison has evidential boundaries.** The *Lettre* conclusion identifies its basis in ancient style and limits its extension to modern style; it requests correction from deeper study (V 306–308). Kawi's activity and formative-thought passages are useful later controls, but they must not be inserted as the earlier fragment's own wording. Particularly, the language/spirit identity formula on VII/1: 46 is in a **deleted variant**, while the active Ergon/Energeia passage is separate. Evidence: PRIMARY-B08–16; C15–18.

## Focused secondary reading

| Work | Newly read extent | What it contributes |
|---|---|---|
| Manchester 1985 | Chapters 4and6: 67–90, 103–124; absent surrounding endnotes not claimed | General relations versus variable realization; relational form/matter; reconstructed national character; limits of causal priority |
| Underhill 2009 | 53–80 and glossary 147–153 | Speech/activity, inherited reshaping, world-view distinctions, cultivation, and clearly attributed later analytical vocabulary |
| Trabant 2023 | Complete supplied chapter 308–325 and footnotes | Cognition and reciprocal address, procedures versus rigid classes, character in use, historical ranking and deterministic reception |
| Nord 2006 | Targeted 131–135, 139–143 | Purpose and readership, documentary/instrumental orientation, separate explanation, loyalty; **not** Nord 1997 book |

Manchester emphasizes character as an explanatory disposition partly shaping form; Trabant emphasizes specificity achieved in usage and texts. The excerpts support a difference of emphasis and explanatory center, not a clean demonstrated contradiction between exclusively prior essence and exclusively subsequent product (SECONDARY-06–07, 17).

Underhill's Weltansicht/Weltanschauung distinction is a strong warning against ideology as an automatic gloss. His glossary describes a distinction inspired by Humboldt, rather than proving an invariant lexical opposition across all Humboldt's writing (SECONDARY-08, 14). Terms attributed to Meschonnic or introduced by Underhill remain reception vocabulary.

All three accounts resist language as transport of finished thoughts, while retaining communication and the other's activity. Neither this agreement nor a model-reader consensus determines the meaning of an individual *Grundzüge* passage. No modern secondary passage is quoted in the new evidence records; precise locators and paraphrases are retained.

## Historical dictionary check

Thirteen exact entries were retrieved and consulted from the [BSB's digital Adelung, Vienna 1811 edition](https://lexika.digitale-sammlungen.de/adelung/online/angebot): Verkehr, Abschleifen, Bilden, Bildung, Ausbilden, Form, Geist, Kraft, Begriff, Anschauen, Ansicht, Wohl and Bis. Local HTML originals, hashes, article URLs and extracted reading copies are recorded in [the retrieval record](../../sources/terminology/lexicon-retrieval.json). BSB warns of possible capture errors; these readings are **digital-entry checks**, not scan-collated quotations.

The entries support a lexical range: Verkehr includes broader social dealings; abschleifen includes removal and smoothing through grinding; bilden includes physical shaping and the formation of faculties; ausbilden has completion/cultivation senses. They therefore support the conditional rules in T06,T27,T28 without deciding every Humboldt occurrence.

The lexical Geist and Kraft ranges resist a single compulsory spirit or physical-force rendering. Anschauen is not restricted to literal looking; the Ansicht entry does not settle Humboldt's grammatical view. Wohl admits modal, intensive, approximate and concessive uses. Bis includes temporal endpoints with bis auf; this shows availability, not necessity, of §1's temporal interpretation. No exact Typus headword was returned by one bounded headword search; this is not proof of absence from all period lexica.

The dated Humboldt contexts above supply actual authorial usage. The dictionary supplies period lexical possibilities. Secondary scholarship supplies interpretations. These three kinds of evidence stay separately labelled in [the register](terminology-register.md) and [evidence records](../../sources/terminology/evidence.json).
