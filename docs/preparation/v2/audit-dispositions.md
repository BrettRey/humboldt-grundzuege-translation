## F01: grammatische Form

Record the strict audible criterion and its independently anchored fixed-word-order condition at 469–470. Narrow the 453 use to analyst recoverability. The final late-reader return already contains the strict use; the audit saw an earlier partial packet, so the finding is not evidence that the final record lacked it.

## F02: Andeutung / Bezeichnung

Retain the grammatical indication use and distinguish substantive-concept designation from relation designation. Add explicit cross-links. The final late-reader return supplies the missing local evidence; the audit packet was partial.

## F03: Form

Group twelve contextual uses into four working groups while retaining every local use and its own rule. Supply the Stoff antecedent at 402 and separately anchored continuation at 438–439. Keep the law/direction/procedure stipulation local to §136: its position does not establish a universal definition of all earlier Form uses.

## F04: Satz / Periode

Default to sentence for the Satz/Periode comparison and rebuild Periode from the fuller 456 passage. Retain proposition/assertion for the independently evidenced preceding-assertions use at 433; the objection does not warrant a blanket ban on that rendering.

## F05: Hinzudenken

Expand the 463 quotation and label the three negative safeguards as translator cautions rather than source assertions. Link related tacit-grammar/introduced-into-language discussions without identifying the expressions.

## F06: Symbol

Add the active main-text statement at 429 that natural form remains independently substantial. It follows the 428 page supplied to the auditor; its existence defeats the apparatus-only inference. Add the 455 inseparability condition and label that expression a contextual/metaphorical use, not proof of a second technical doctrine.

## F07: Zeichen

Expand 461 to include the parallel replacement of form by concept; define this local opposition without importing §147’s strict criterion or asserting a contribution absent from the passage.

## F08: Verstand

Merge faculty activities as contextual uses; merge the qualifying sense idioms separately. Include faculty use im Verstande des Redenden at 462 and the idiom at 451. The string im Verstande alone is not a sense diagnostic.

## F09: Vermoegen

Retain one headword with explicitly bearer-conditioned English: faculties of the soul at 421, capacity of a language at 434. Quote both passages; neither the local contrast nor these English choices establish a universal Kraft/Vermögen taxonomy.

## F10: Weltansicht

State that the individual standpoint is possible independently of language and is intensified through it. Merge the individual/language bearer uses under one working sense; do not infer shared ideology.

## F11: Denkform / Gedankenform (and Sprachform)

Link Denkform and Gedankenform as near-equivalent related compounds without inventing a doctrinal opposition. They remain distinct German headwords, rather than being treated as inflections of one word. Merge the generic and specifically linguistic Sprachform uses while retaining the local conditions.

## F12: EXCLUSION SAMPLE: early:0025 Allein; early:0658 Mensch; early:0659 Menschen

Replace the misleading ordinary-word category boilerplate, especially for ordinary nouns. Record selection at candidate-form/page level. The final report expressly disclaims a fresh sense annotation of every occurrence; one audit window per candidate cannot establish such coverage or demonstrate that the exclusions are false.

## Limits

The auditor’s agreement is not source verification. In particular, its apparatus-only inference for Symbol and proposed universal Form definition were not adopted. New source windows and revised grouping were coordinator/reader checks, not another independent model round. Source-critical questions, the unresolved printed448 Satz phrase and specialist validation remain as stated in the [coverage report](coverage-report.md).
