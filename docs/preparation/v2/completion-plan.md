## Records and evidence

Create separate records for source terms and compounds, with contextual senses where the meaning changes. Inflected forms and spelling variants belong to a term record rather than inflating its headword count. Keep separate terms linked through explicit contrasts, compounds and related concepts. A change of bearer alone does not necessarily constitute a new lexical sense.

Each operational sense needs a local primary passage containing an actual occurrence, exact page/PDF/scan location, a short source excerpt, English candidate(s), a definition of the local role, a rendering rule and a stated exception or limit. Supporting historical-dictionary and secondary evidence is attached only where it actually concerns that term/sense. Group-level references are not copied indiscriminately to every member.

Quote anchoring, source-layer attribution, and interpretive judgement are different checks. Preserve the uncorrected OCR. Compare load-bearing, ambiguous and corrupt readings with page images; do not silently fix a quote to make it anchor. A term appearing only in a parallel or policy source is labelled as such, not counted as a Grundzüge headword.

## Coverage method

Three source readers revisit printed364–399,400–444 and445–473. They select consequential terminology from every page: definitions, explicit contrasts, philosophical/psychological vocabulary, linguistic operations, grammatical categories, sound/prosodic terms, classificatory language and consequential historical/metaphorical vocabulary. They also inspect a reproducible inventory of capitalized German-form candidates and selected technical lower-case families. The inventory is deliberately broad and includes inflections, ordinary words, examples, names, apparatus and OCR noise.

Every inventory candidate receives a disposition: included/variant with a target term, ordinary nontechnical use, cited example, proper name/language label, apparatus-only, or OCR noise, with unresolved cases retained for coordinator review. The full-text page review supplies phrases and rare terms the mechanical inventory misses. The coordinator checks remaining candidates, old-register carry-forward, sense support and cross-section consistency. Completion is not an entry-count target and is not universal word-by-word semantic annotation.

## Acceptance and stopping rule

- Every main-text page has a recorded terminology review.
- Every mechanical candidate has a reviewed disposition, or a named source problem with an explicit consequence; no unexamined remainder is quietly excluded.
- Every original register label maps to a term, variant, phrase or explicit scope exclusion.
- Every operational sense has occurrence-specific primary evidence, with its source layer and verification status.
- Distinctive senses and translation restrictions are retained; conflicting interpretations have dispositions rather than a model vote.
- A bounded independent omission/semantic audit is completed and material findings resolved.
- The relational store and reader-facing termbase agree, source/span/foreign-key checks pass, and preserved inputs match their baseline hashes.

Uncertifiable individual examples, the unavailable critical edition/manuscript and the need for specialist human validation remain stated limits. They do not justify another claim of exhaustive lexical certification. The final report must state what was actually reviewed and what remains unusable for exact quotation.
