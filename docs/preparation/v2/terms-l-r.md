### 3. Laut

**English:** sound / speech sound.

Audible linguistic expression considered in its correspondence to conceptual determinations.

**Rule:** Not automatically a modern phoneme, written letter or mere nonlinguistic noise.

**Evidence (direct occurrence):** [GS V:457 / PDF 467 / scan leaf 471](../../../sources/raw/gs-v-1906.pdf#page=467); main text; image-checked short quotation.

> den Laut überall dem Begriff gleichzustellen.

**Comparative control:** [printed 59–60; 69; PDF 72–73; 82; §8, Sprache; 9, The Work of the Mind](../../../sources/raw/references/underhill-2009-humboldt-worldview-language.pdf). Underhill emphasizes transient speaking acts, audible expression and the speaker’s hearing of the word. Writing preserves an incomplete representation that must be returned to living utterance. A comparative control for this named term; the individual local sense is supported by its own primary attestation.
