# Humboldt terminology: individual terms and contextual uses

This termbase gives separate records for source terms, compounds, phrases and consequential contextual vocabulary. Observed inflections and spelling variants are listed within a record. Contextual uses are distinguished where the local contribution or contrast changes; their number is not a claim about a fixed dictionary polysemy. All proposed English remains provisional.

It contains **1,048 headword records, 1,518 contextual sense/use groups and 1,566 primary evidence records**. These include contextual vocabulary; several records can cite the same passage. Supporting translation-policy terms are labelled separately. The earlier 34-group register remains preserved as a historical input.

[Coverage and accuracy report](v2/coverage-report.md) · [Store guide](v2/store-guide.md) · [Structured termbase](../../sources/terminology/v2/termbase.json)

## Find a term

Use the alphabetical index below or search by headword/observed form in SQLite. The detailed records give the English choices, local definition, rendering rule, caution, checked quotation and exact source locator. The source images and original OCR remain unchanged.

Some consequential choices to consult first:

- [Form](v2/terms-f-k.md#form): what has form, and its local relation to matter or activity.
- [Verstand](v2/terms-s-z.md#verstand) and [Vermögen](v2/terms-s-z.md#vermoegen): faculty, idiom and bearer-conditioned English.
- [Grammatical form](v2/terms-f-k.md#grammatische-form) and [Hinzudenken](v2/terms-f-k.md#hinzudenken): audible indication versus supplying a relation in thought.
- [Satz](v2/terms-s-z.md#satz), [Periode](v2/terms-l-r.md#periode) and [Weltansicht](v2/terms-s-z.md#weltansicht): local units and viewpoints without imported modern equations.

## Reading the evidence

Checked wording is kept in a separate excerpt collection from the OCR locator window. An image check is model-assisted source verification, not independent specialist validation. Quoted content is an attestation for the specified local interpretation, not evidence that every matching token has that sense.

Historical-dictionary entries and primary/secondary parallels are comparative controls. They are attached explicitly where relevant, rather than copied from an old group onto all its members. A dictionary range does not override Humboldt’s local contrast.

A candidate disposition reports why a surface form was included, treated as a variant, or excluded from terminology. It does not silently remove that word from the searchable source or certify all of its occurrences. The coverage report gives the selection criteria, reviewed remainder, source issues and audit dispositions.
