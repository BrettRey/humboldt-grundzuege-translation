## Consequential choices

- **heading:i1 — Printed hierarchy and presentation.** Retain the checked Arabic1, not the RomanI introduced by Codex. Darstellung is presentation, as in the earlier heading; do not silently change the level.
- **heading:A — Grundzüge and general type.** Use Outlines consistently with the work’s title. Typus concerns the general process pattern, not a morphological language class.
- **heading:a — Scope of the printed heading.** Keep language in general, not a claim about a single language.
- **p17:s3 — Interaction without added constituent ontology.** Full interaction preserves Zusammenwirken without adding the base candidate’s explicit parts where the German does not name them.
- **p18:s1 — Provisional organizational deduction.** Keep might follow, the chapter-wide material and at least in outline; do not turn the proposal into an established classification.
- **p19:s1 — Thought and feeling capacity.** Render the coordinated Denk- und Empfindungsvermögen without requiring two separately enumerated faculties.
- **p21:s11 — Historical evaluation.** Mutilated retains verstümmelt rather than silently softening the author’s judgment. The focused sign-language note supplies the modern qualification.
- **p23:s1 — Bodily sensation and expressive feeling.** Sensation fits the animal-cry context; feeling remains appropriate in the wider thought/feeling contrast. No single English equivalent is imposed on every Empfindung.
- **p25:s2 — Wesen in context.** Nature states the character of the thinking process, avoiding a new doctrine introduced by stylistic variation.
- **p25:s7 — Testing intelligibility.** Keep the experimental testing claim without implying a specific chronological experiment before all self-understanding.
- **p27:s6 — Qualification of the singing creature.** Retain the active qualification only one that joins ideas to its tones, within the singing comparison, alongside the qualified historical label and richness assertion.
- **p28:s5 — Ideenlos is not inattentiveness.** Ringing devoid of ideas retains the claimed lack of determinate ideational effect, not an ordinary accusation of careless speech; the following allowance for feeling remains.
- **p29:s2 — Audible identity and classification.** Retain the distinction between increasingly close classification and a language expressing its own singular identity to the ear.
- **p29:s5 — Uncertain printed verb and stock.** The enlarged print confirms ab-schliesst. Brings much to a close leaves completion/closing-off less narrowly decided; both remain possible. Do not silently emend to a wear-away verb. Stock preserves the grouping metaphor without adding a modern genetic theory.
- **p31:s7 — Representing as an activity.** The moving mass is of Vorstellen, not merely a collection of already finished representations.
- **p31:s17 — Shared action through influence.** Keep the compressed causal/shared-action relation; mutual influence is plausible in context but reciprocal direction is not explicit in this phrase.
- **p33:s3 — Common active power in comprehension.** Power of language keeps Sprachkraft consistent; understanding activates inward receptivity, not a passive store of meanings.
- **p33:s6 — Nested determination and inner life.** Keep stock, nation, dialect, established sound signs, use and ultimate individuality as nested or successive determinations; inner life is Gemüth, not a new separate cognitive faculty.
- **p35:s3 — Printed supplementation and the kinship genitive.** Render the printed [zu] as an ordinary English infinitive, recording the supplementation separately. Retain the difficult kinship of human individuality without inventing an independently evidenced metaphysics.
- **p36:s1 — Target of the objection.** This opposite view refers to the first account in34, not the favoured unity of nature. Keep understanding as active awakening of the hearer’s power of language.
- **p36:s4 — Animal sensation and human linguistic power.** The activation contrast is not a denial that even the earliest child may already have linguistic power; preserve the parenthetical qualification.
- **p36:s8 — Growth rather than memorized deposits.** Capacity for language renders Sprachvermögen; the meting-out/deposit/babbling metaphor remains distinct from growth through age and practice.
- **p36:s11 — Direction and strength of the developmental inference.** The observations are offered as evidence of development rather than mechanical learning, not as conclusions proved by it. Preserve the universal timing assertion and qualify it in a sourced note.
- **p37:s2 — Earliest transfer and continued universal capacity.** Restore the mother’s-lap image. The German does not specify a precise measured transfer age; the shared human capacity claim and its contrast with diversification remain.
- **p37:s6 — Stock-like disposition.** Keep the historical claim concerning disposition without translating it into modern genetic terminology.
- **p37:s9 — Predisposition is asserted, not established.** Use they had originally been destined for another language, but this destiny had been accidentally altered. This takes zu einer andren Sprache with Bestimmung; attachment to verrückt, meaning shifted onto another language, remains possible and is disclosed. No named individual case is supplied.
- **p39:s6 — Weltansicht and standpoint.** Keep view of the world recoverable rather than replacing it with an unrelated ideological label. It is a standpoint of subjective apprehension.
- **p39:s7 — Three components of the concept.** Retain impression, subjective reception and the word as speech sound as three contributions. External object clarifies the Gegenstand’s referent here without establishing a global physical-thing/Object ontology.
- **p40:s9 — Both opposed predicates belong to language.** Language itself is object/independent and subject/dependent in precisely the same respect; do not reduce Subject to an English adjective subjective.
- **p42:s4 — Unexpressed noun in many.** Many cases leaves open whether generations or languages supply the ellipsis. Remove merely, which could imply that imperceptibility is the only issue everywhere.
- **p43:s5 — Descent, transformation and author note.** Retain the warning against a language emerging as a separate complete object. The mother language transforms through successive speakers and mixture. Store the original callout after Sprachen and render the full author note separately, not as a raw asterisk in the body.
- **p46:s6 — Distributive language.** Their languages makes clear the two stocks relate to two primordial languages; it does not turn the singular German into a claim of one shared language.
- **p47:s6 — Appositional sounds.** Read als Töne as specifying the means, namely sounds, not an added contrast of language using means as sounds.
- **p48:s7 — Author’s methodological self-check.** Restore the person who risks exaggeration rather than personifying general reasoning as surrendering itself.
- **p49:s1 — Avoid separating individuality and similarity into domains.** The rejection covers the allocation of individuality solely to sound and similarity solely to the ideal sphere; do not imply the second allocation is independently endorsed.
- **p50:s6 — Not-understanding is within understanding.** Retain the parallel formulation; failure to understand might suggest total communicative failure, whereas the following argument treats degrees of divergence.
- **p52:s8 — Intellectualitaet without modern IQ.** Use intellectual life, as in54, for Intellectualitaet; latitude, as in51, for Spielraum. Neither word entails national IQ or a separate measured mechanism.
- **p54:s3 — Dead versus living metaphor.** Retain dead language as the contrast with the living mind and in55. Here dead is inherited material abstracted from living acts, not necessarily an extinct language.
- **p54:s5 — Orientation towards intellectual life.** The pronoun refers to national individuality; preserve both whether/which direction and specifically through language. Intellectual life renders the same German family as52 without introducing measured national intelligence.
- **p55:s1 — Retrospective duality and references.** Keep act/material and natural interconnectedness/freedom distinctions and all printed numeric cross-references, including the overlap at51; no silent correction.
- **p55:s2 — Words and combining into discourse.** Make Redeverbindung intelligible in English while retaining its stated matter/form relation, not forcing a modern syntactic category on the whole term.
- **p55:s3 — Matter/form is not sounds/rules.** Preserve both counterexamples and discourse as purpose; word formation also has rules and connected discourse can employ distinctive sounds.
- **p20:s7 — Compressed coordination and hypothetical origin.** Take intellectual activity as the subject of establishes; articulated sound is the competing grammatical subject. Put the hypothetical beginning with establishment, but disclose the subject and scope alternatives in a local note. Preserve sound becoming articulated sound without a false invariant Ton/Laut equivalence.
- **p22:s2 — Essential nature and preceding logical account.** Supply nature from the preceding clause, but retain the author’s claim of dependence on vibration; do not reconcile it with21 by a distinction absent from the text.
- **p30:s2 — Two announced aspects.** Supply second as English coordination for the twofold announcement; the third higher unity is left unexplained, as in the source.
- **p31:s12 — Wesenheit and a hearer.** Reality, not substantial reality, retains the word’s objective standing through hearing/reply without silently adding material substance.
- **p31:s19 — Pronominal form across languages.** Retain Humboldt’s structural universality claim without translating it as phonetic identity or genealogical proof. The pronouns are I/you/he, with you representing singular Du.
- **p34:s4 — Inclusive origin parenthesis.** Read the uneven nicht … sondern auch inclusively as not just … but also, guided by auch. Disclose the supplied scope; retain the modest inner-conviction framing and the unity-of-nature contrast.
- **p38:s3 — Inca identification limit.** Keep the source’s unspecified language of the Incas and both family/rank associations; do not equate it with a named modern variety without evidence.
- **p44:s3 — Printed pronoun and the overpowered party.** Retain [they] for print-supplied [sie]; the singular participle describes the one party overpowered physically then mentally, not the newly convergent language.
- **p45:s1 — Unity without one primordial language.** The unity is mediated sharing among connected languages; retain the explicit denial that this means one original ancestor.
- **p47:s3 — Hypothesis rather than migration fact.** Preserve the supposition and its consequence: no similarities due to previously formed language under the stipulated absence of contact; do not turn that supposition into established history.
- **p49:s2 — Weltvorstellung differs lexically from Weltansicht.** Use representation of the world for the changed term without manufacturing a fully separate theory.
- **p49:s5 — Admission about demonstration and repeated because.** Retain the inability to demonstrate the whole relation empirically in a particular language, followed by the theoretical and practical reasons; preserve the resumed if-clause readably.
- **p50:s16 — Sounds, not silently words.** The antecedent Laute is retained even though personal dictionary points to word sounds/spoken forms. Record the breadth rather than changing the source noun to words.
- **p51:s2 — Reversed demonstratives and physiological analogy.** Assign physiological operation to language’s power over humans, dynamic operation to human power over language. Keep freedom’s pronoun open at the level of language/its operative powers; the note explains the analogy.
- **p52:s6 — National aggregate referent.** Take in demselben as in national influence rather than in the individual; disclose the alternative because it changes the account of aggregation.
- **note:klaproth — Author footnote and printed page brackets.** Translate the complete note, retain the source-supplied [43.] and attribution to Humboldt; no claim that Klaproth’s cited page has been independently checked.
- **p16:s2 — General type as the comparison point.** The type is the general pattern of the mental process to which individual descriptions must return; it is not a preassigned morphological class. Now avoids adding an explicit therefore inference.

## Audits and dispositions

**claude (claude-fable-5-2):** Coverage and method. All 281 IDs were read against the checked German blocks as one connected argument (16–55): 277 main-text blocks, three headings and the author footnote. There are 57 findings: none major, 8 moderate, 49 minor. The other 224 IDs are marked checked, meaning I found no reportable issue, not that they are certified. Nothing from 56 onward was translated, and proposed English is limited to the affected phrases.

Overall fidelity. The translation is accurate and readable. I found no block where the main proposition is reversed or materially falsified, and no omitted or invented sentences. Parenthetical cross-references, the editorial '[sie]' in 44, and Humboldt's hedges are generally preserved. Splitting long periods into several English sentences does not, with the exceptions listed, change scope or modality.

Main systematic weakness: terminology across the unit. Several inconsistencies sit on either side of 36–40, which may reflect the two drafting blocks.
- Gewalt, Macht and Kraft all become 'power', which erases the opposition drawn in 51 (F53, F38).
- Gegenstand and Object both become 'object', except for a one-off 'thing' in 31 (F24, F35).
- 'geistig' is sometimes 'intellectual', overlapping with 'intellectuell' (F19).
- Empfindung alternates between 'sensation' and 'feeling', overlapping with Gefühl (F05, F51).
- Zusammenwirken is 'interaction' in one place and 'cooperation' in another, the first overlapping with Wechselwirkung (F04, F21).
- Stufe, Spielraum, Intellectualität, Stamm, Anregung, and Menschengeschlecht/Menschheit each have two renderings (F27, F54–F56, F41, F26, F30).
- Ton and Laut are both 'sound'. This is defensible, since Humboldt's own usage is fluid, but it blurs the sentence where Ton becomes Laut (F07).
A short fixed term list applied across both drafting blocks would remove most of the minor findings.

Source ambiguity versus error. The following are ambiguities or irregularities in the German that the English resolves silently; they are not demonstrable mistranslations: the subject of 'begründet' and the scope of the parenthesis in p20:s7; 'abschliesst' in p29:s5; the attachment of 'zu einer andren Sprache' in p37:s9; 'in vielen' in p42:s4; 'nur innerhalb eines gewissen Spielraums' in p51:s4; 'in einem gewissen Punkte' in p43:s7; and the missing 'bloss' in p34:s4. I could not settle these from the supplied material and report them as open. Smaller unflagged cases where the translator's choice seems reasonable include 'irgend einen' in p41:s5, 'sie' in p40:s10 and 'seiner' in p33:s3.

Source status. The checked 1906 blocks were treated as governing; the uncorrected whole-work OCR was used only as background (for example, for the footnote anchor) and never to override a checked reading. Where the two differ, the translation follows the checked text, correctly: 'feiner' in p28:s7, 'Leben' in p50:s6, 'Stätiges' in p24:s2, and the numeral '1.' in heading:i1. The checked block p46:s3 reads 'Verstände', apparently for 'Verstande'; the translation's 'sense' is right, but the transcription should be rechecked. The footnote anchor and the bracketed page number need confirming against the edition (F44).

Fidelity is not endorsement. Faithful rendering of Humboldt's assertions is not confirmation that they are true. This applies to his remarks on 'so-called savages', deaf-mutes, 'races', women's dialects in American languages, the Inca language, descent and the vocal organs, a flood, and the possible isolation of America. The period vocabulary ('deaf-mutes', 'savages', 'races') is kept as historical wording; any framing is for the edition's apparatus. The tension between p29:s1 (individuality lies 'allein' in sound) and p49:s1 ('nicht bloss in ihrem Ton') is the author's, and the translation rightly preserves it.

Limits. This is a single-pass audit done without tools, outside research, or delegation, as instructed. It is not a specialist human certification. The German of the moderate findings in particular should be reviewed by a Humboldt specialist before the text is finalized.

- **claude/F01 — minor; p16:s1.** Unclear referent. 'einander so gleich und ungleich' compares the powers, means and results of one language with those of another; the English 'its powers, means and results resemble and differ from one another' can be read as comparing powers with means with results. The possessive 'its' is also an addition. **Disposition:** Accepted: remove its and make the comparison from language to language explicit; powers are not being compared with means or results as different categories.
- **claude/F02 — minor; p16:s2.** 'nun' is rendered 'therefore', which adds an inferential link the German does not assert. **Disposition:** Accepted: now replaces therefore, avoiding an unnecessarily explicit deduction.
- **claude/F03 — minor; p16:s3.** 'bedingt' is rendered 'determined', the word used everywhere else for 'bestimmt'. **Disposition:** Accepted: conditioned retains the local bedingt/bestimmt difference.
- **claude/F04 — minor; p17:s3.** 'in seinem ganzen Zusammenwirken' becomes 'in its full interaction'. 'Interaction' is the rendering of 'Wechselwirkung' elsewhere (23, 39, 54), and 'Zusammenwirken' becomes 'cooperation' in p30:s4. The English also leaves it unclear what interacts with what. **Disposition:** Partly accepted: combined operation as a whole clarifies Zusammenwirken and avoids the Wechselwirkung rendering interaction. A compulsory invariant noun is not imposed:17 concerns the procedure’s combined operation,30 combined social action upon language.
- **claude/F05 — minor; p19:s1.** Inconsistent rendering of one concept. 'Empfindungsvermögen' is 'capacity for ... feeling' here but 'faculty of sensation' in p36:s4. More broadly, 'Empfindung/empfinden' alternates between 'sensation' (p23:s1, p26:s1, p36:s4) and 'feeling' (p19:s1, p27:s2, p31:s6, p39:s12, p49:s5, p49:s7, p50:s5, p50:s7), where it merges with 'Gefühl' = 'feeling'. **Disposition:** Not adopted as a universal substitution. The checked uses contrast thinking/feeling in19, but animal sensory responsiveness in23/26/36. Sensation in all uses would impose a narrower bodily concept on the former. The conditional termbase permits feeling/sensation; the guide exposes the overlapping Gefühl/Empfindung renderings.
- **claude/F06 — minor; p20:s5.** 'wirkt ... wieder bestimmend auf den Geist zurück' is rendered 'in turn acts upon and determines the mind'. This drops the 'acts back' wording used for 'zurückwirken' everywhere else (p19:s2, p20:s7, p34:s1, p40:s4, p42:s4). **Disposition:** Accepted: restore acts back upon the mind, determining it, making the recurring feedback relation explicit.
- **claude/F07 — moderate; p20:s7.** Three linked points, the first two source ambiguities that the English resolves silently. (1) The subject of 'begründet' is ambiguous: 'sie' (intellectual activity) or 'er' (the sound now become articulated). The English assigns it to intellectual activity; the nearer subject is the sound. (2) The parenthesis '(wenn es möglich wäre, einen Anfang aller Sprache zu denken)' stands between 'Laut' and 'begründet' and most naturally qualifies the founding of a domain of sounds. The English encloses it in dashes with the becoming-articulated clause, shifting its scope. (3) 'Ton ... wird zum articulirten Laut' becomes 'sound becomes articulated sound'; the Ton/Laut step is carried only by the adjective. **Disposition:** Partly accepted: move the hypothetical condition to the establishment clause and remove the dash enclosure that confined it to articulation. Retain intellectual activity as subject of establishes, while a new local note records articulated sound as the alternative subject. Sound/articulated sound carries the contextual Ton/Laut distinction without inventing an invariant tone/speech-sound lexicon.
- **claude/F08 — minor; p21:s2.** 'der Form seines Wirkens' becomes 'the form of its own activity'. 'Its' is ambiguous in English between mind and sound, and 'Wirken' is rendered 'activity', the standing equivalent of 'Thätigkeit' ('Wirken' is 'operation' in heading:i1, p51:s2 and p52:s9). **Disposition:** Accepted: identify the mind as possessor of the operation; operation keeps Wirken visible without a rigid distinction from all activity.
