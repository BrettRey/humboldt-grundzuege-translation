## claude (claude-fable-5-2)

For a time I wavered between two plans: whether to let the publication of the individual grammars and dictionaries come first, or the presentation of the general conclusions drawn from the investigations into the individual languages.

This latter procedure has the disadvantage that the reader, for whom the aids to the study of the individual languages must first be created, given the scarcity and the often very inconvenient arrangement of those that exist, is in a position to test the correctness of the general assertions only very incompletely.

But I was deterred from the former procedure by the protractedness of the task, and by the length of time that, given the difficulty of publishing such writings, would necessarily have had to elapse over it.
