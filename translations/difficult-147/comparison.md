# §147 comparison and pilot assessment

One bounded comparison/audit/revision cycle is complete. The selected section is harder than §§1–2 because a stipulated criterion must survive dense syntax, an independent-anchor condition, shifting pronouns, a historical Chinese example and a limit on what can be inferred from present language. [Read the revised English](revised.md); [the source packet](packet.md) contains the complete checked German.

## Candidate and source coverage

Three isolated source-only candidates completed successfully through runtime models `claude-fable-5-2`, `muse-wonder-full-1.0` and `gpt-6.1-sol`. Their 18 sentence renderings each were saved and hashed before synthesis. They saw no old English, glossary answers or another candidate. The coordinator subsequently consulted the source and termbase, preserving every source sentence and important condition. All 18 source sentences have rendering links in each candidate, synthesis and revision. Nine consequential span links and six selected termbase-rule associations are additional proposed analysis, not a full syntactic parse.

All three candidates preserved the basic argument. Their agreement did not settle the pronoun in s07, the extension of Nomina, the graphic identity of ì or the best English for Sach- vocabulary. Those remain explicit local judgments or source limits.

## What this pilot establishes

The workflow can produce a traceable, readable provisional treatment of a difficult section and expose a real scope loss introduced during synthesis. The termbase supplies useful conditional constraints; fresh candidates and source-based audits add checks that vocabulary alone cannot supply. The database preserves words, sentences, selected clauses/phrases, alternatives, decisions and evidence rather than storing only one English paragraph.

This is evidence from one section. It does not estimate an error rate, establish that every remaining section is manageable, or validate Humboldt’s account of Chinese. The footnote’s terminal 98, the historical transcription’s phonetic/character identity, the pronoun alternative and manuscript finality remain stated limits. None is silently repaired. The critical-edition gap remains under the declared 1906 base policy; §§1–7’s reported cancellation is not applied to §147.

Existing translations and the sealed terminology snapshot remain unchanged. Stop after §147; no §148 or sequential §3 was drafted. English review is pending.
