## Claude — claude-fable-5-2

Now the striking similarity of the American languages also speaks in favour of this. In the cultivation of the nations to which they belong there have certainly been significant differences, unmistakable also in the languages themselves; yet the lack of alphabetic writing, and hence of literature proper, nonetheless sets all of them, without exception, equal on a lower level. The sameness of their structure, which has sometimes been falsely asserted, does indeed vanish before more deeply penetrating research, just as does the deviation from the structure of all other languages, which has at times also been asserted; but all the languages of America do really present great features of similarity, and the north and south of that part of the world do not even allow truly separating boundaries to be drawn in this respect.
